Profile

A Taste of Nature’s Luxury

At Oxundus, we bring you the purest dry fruits, sourced directly from nature’s finest farms. Our mission is to deliver health, flavor, and tradition—all packed into every bite. Whether you’re snacking smart, gifting someone special, or fueling your day with nutrition — we’re here to serve you with excellence.

Business Overview

For 70 years, our family has awakened senses with Afghanistan’s most exquisite dry fruits.Each golden apricot, plump raisin, and crisp almond tells a story of sun-drenched valleys and time-honored craftsmanship. Today, we bridge continents – from Kandahar’s fertile soils to tables in London and New York. Our third- generation artisans combine ancestral techniques with modern quality standards, creating unparalleled taste experiences. We don’t just sell dry fruits;

we share heirloom recipes of nature, perfected through decades of passion. Every bite carries the warmth of Afghan sunshine and our family’s unwavering commitment to excellence.

Established in 2008

Rooted in Nature. Grown with Care.

At oxundus, our journey began in 2008 with a mission to bring nature’s purest dried fruits to your table. From humble beginnings as a family-owned farm-to-market brand, we’ve grown into a trusted name in dried fruits — loved for our dedication to quality, health, and honesty.

Our Early Days

Harvested by Hand, Loved by Many

What started as a small-scale operation founded by Dzung Nova was fueled by a passion for healthy living and natural ingredients. Every fruit was handpicked, sun-dried, and packed with care — a commitment that still guides us today.

Growing with You

From Local Stalls to Global Homes

As word spread about our flavorful and nutritious products, we expanded — launching our first online store and retail partnerships. This allowed us to reach families across the globe while staying true to our roots in natural farming and slow drying.

Governance Principles

At Oxundus, governance is more than compliance — it is the foundation of trust we build with our customers, partners, and team. Rooted in the values of transparency, fairness, and ethical conduct, our governance framework guides every decision, from farm-level sourcing to global distribution.

We believe that good governance begins with clarity. Our leadership team ensures that internal policies, financial practices, and business strategies reflect not only modern corporate standards but also the integrity inherited from three generations of family tradition. We uphold autonomy in our board structure, allowing space for independent judgment, diverse perspectives, and responsible oversight.
